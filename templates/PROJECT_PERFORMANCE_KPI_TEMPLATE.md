- `Time improvement % = ((baseline hours - actual hours) / baseline hours) × 100`
- Compare only equivalent milestone definitions.
- Mark reconstructed or estimated time clearly.
- If baseline is unavailable or zero, use `N/A`.
- Do not claim efficiency gain by bypassing quality, security, backup, Preview or Production gates.
